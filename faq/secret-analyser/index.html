Secret Analyser

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Analyser do?

Secret Analyser shows your whole mix at a glance. You see your tracks together, spot where they crowd each other and can keep a favourite song beside your mix for comparison. That way you make your next move with more confidence.

Is Secret Analyser free, and how do I get it?

Secret Analyser is free. No purchase or license key is needed. As soon as the download is open on the product page, request the download link by email there. You do not need to subscribe to the newsletter.

What do I need for Secret Analyser?

You need a Mac with macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel, and music software that supports AU or VST3. Secret Analyser is not yet available for Windows.

How do I set up Secret Analyser in my project?

Put Secret Analyser on every track you want to see and choose Sender there. Put one more instance at the very end of your master track and choose Analyser there. That is where you see all tracks together.

Why do the values not change when I move the track fader?

The track fader does not change these values. Change the volume before the Sender instead, for example with a simple gain plugin in front of it. Then the values stay right.

A track does not show up in the Analyser. What can I check?

Use the same format everywhere: either VST3 everywhere or AU everywhere. Check that the Sender on that track is switched on. If your music software runs each plugin separately, for example Bitwig, the tracks cannot see each other yet.

How do I see only one group, for example the vocals?

Solo the group in your music software and pick it in the Analyser. The values then show only that group, the way it finally sounds.

How do I report a problem with Secret Analyser?

Open the View menu in the plugin and choose Report a problem or Report bug / Wish. You can also write to support@secret.vip directly. Include the version, your operating system and what happened. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files.

How do I install Secret Analyser?

Secret Analyser is a free Mac plugin and is not released for download yet. Once it is available, the download will be on the product page.

Do I need to activate Secret Analyser?

Secret Analyser is free. No purchase is needed to download it.

Is Secret Analyser a trial version?

This is a free offer, not a time-limited trial.

How do I remove Secret Analyser?

Close your music software and remove the plugin from your plugin folders.

What is the difference to Secret Analyser Beta?

“Secret Analyser Beta” is a separate version with its own answers in the help. Please pick the matching plugin in the product list.
