Secret Future EQ 2

Short answers to the most common questions.

How do I install the plugin on Mac?

Download the Mac version at https://plugins.secret.vip/download/, open the .pkg file and follow the steps. Then restart your DAW or rescan plugins.

How do I install the plugin on Windows?

Download the Windows version at https://plugins.secret.vip/download/ and unzip it. Copy VST3\SecretFutureEQ2.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and CLAP\SecretFutureEQ2.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP. Then restart your DAW or rescan plugins.

How do I activate my license?

Open the plugin, paste the license key from your purchase email and click Activate. You do not need an account.

How do I move to a new computer?

You can use the license on two computers at the same time. On the old computer: gear icon → License → Deactivate this computer. Then activate it on the new computer with your key.

Where can I find updates?

Find the current version at https://plugins.secret.vip/download/. Changes are listed at https://plugins.secret.vip/changelog/. Updates within the same major version are free.

How do I request a refund?

Request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Reply to your Paddle receipt email, use its link, or write to support@secret.vip with your order number. Paddle refunds your original payment method; the license key is then deactivated.

What are the system requirements?

Secret Future EQ 2: macOS 12 or newer on Apple Silicon or Intel (VST3, AU, CLAP), or 64-bit Windows 10/11 (VST3, CLAP).

The plugin or license email is missing. What can I check?

If the plugin is missing from your DAW, restart the DAW or rescan plugins. If the license email is missing, check your spam folder. At https://plugins.secret.vip/key/ you can have the key resent to your purchase address. If that does not help, write to support@secret.vip.

I lost my license key. What now?

Enter your purchase email address at https://plugins.secret.vip/key/. Your license key and download links will be resent to that address.

What does the hearing note on first launch mean?

Start with a low monitoring volume. Changes in the plugin can make the sound much louder suddenly. Do not rely on output protection to protect your ears. Always set your monitoring volume deliberately.

What does Secret Future EQ 2 do?

Secret Future EQ 2 helps you quickly shape the sound of your music in the direction you want. You can work with an audio file or live audio. Listen to the result and adjust it to your taste.

How do I report a problem with Secret Future EQ 2?

Use “Report problem” in the plugin if your version offers it. You can also write to support@secret.vip. Include the version, operating system and what happened. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files.

Is there a trial version?

If the plugin shows the button for a free trial, you can try Secret Future EQ 2 without a license key. Without that button there is no trial right now. Every purchase comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Does the plugin work without internet?

Yes. You need internet once, briefly, to activate. After that the plugin confirms your license online about every 30 days. If that is not possible, it keeps working for 14 more days without a connection. Connect to the internet once during that time and open the plugin.

How do I uninstall Secret Future EQ 2?

First free up the computer: gear icon → License → Deactivate this computer. Then close your DAW and delete the SecretFutureEQ2 plugin files. Mac: In the Finder, open your hard drive, then Library → Audio → Plug-Ins; the files are in the VST3, Components and CLAP folders. Windows: in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP.
