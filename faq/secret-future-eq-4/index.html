Secret Future EQ 4

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Future EQ 4 do?

It moves your sound towards a reference you choose. Pick a reference, play your track and press Secret Match: 26 bands adjust your sound. Amount sets how far it goes, Match % shows how close you are. You can add your own bands on top.

Which references can I use in Secret Future EQ 4?

The built-in Secret Curve, an audio file you load or drop on the graph, or a sound you capture while it plays. Save your own references in My Curves.

What does Dynamic Match do in Secret Future EQ 4?

It keeps your sound close to the reference while the music plays. You set amount, range and speed, and you can limit it to a frequency area, for example to tame harsh S sounds. Look-ahead adds latency only while Dynamic Match is on.

How do I compare fairly in Secret Future EQ 4?

Auto-Gain keeps the loudness equal to the input, so bypass compares the tone, not the volume. Three slots A, B and C keep different settings, with undo and redo.

Which formats and systems does Secret Future EQ 4 support?

Mac: VST3, AU and CLAP, macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel. Windows: VST3 and CLAP, Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

On how many computers can I use Secret Future EQ 4?

One license works on two computers at the same time. To move to a new computer, deactivate it on the old one and activate it on the new one.
