Audio Normaliser

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Audio Normaliser do to my files?

Your audio files get a more consistent loudness, making different recordings more comfortable to listen to together.

Is it a plugin for my music software?

No. It is a separate application for audio files.

Where do I download and install Audio Normaliser?

Mac: Unzip the download and move the included app to Applications. Windows: Unzip the download and open the included installer. Follow its steps, then open Audio Normaliser. Open the app directly; you do not need to scan for plugins in your DAW. Keep security protection enabled. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

Do I need a purchase or license key for Audio Normaliser?

No. Audio Normaliser is offered free of charge. Get it directly from our download page; no purchase or license key is needed. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

How do I choose files and the output folder?

Drag a folder or audio files into the app. Check the selected output folder before starting. You can change it in the app. Reprocessing files can replace existing results. Back up important files first.

How do I update Audio Normaliser and report a problem?

Get new packages from our download page. If something goes wrong, tell support what you were trying to do and what message the app shows. Include whether you use Mac or Windows and the version you used. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

What does Audio Normaliser require?

The Mac package reviewed here requires macOS 12 or later and a Mac with Apple Silicon. This package does not contain an Intel version. The Windows package contains a 64-bit app. Audio Normaliser is a separate app; you do not need a DAW. The minimum supported Windows version has not yet been confirmed for this download package. Windows requires Microsoft Edge WebView2 and Windows runtime components.

Is Audio Normaliser a trial version?

No. Audio Normaliser is free and not a time-limited trial. You do not need a purchase or a license key.

Can I use Audio Normaliser on a second computer?

Yes. Audio Normaliser does not need activation. Download the app on the other computer and install it the same way. The two-computer limit only applies to purchased licenses.

Does Audio Normaliser work without internet?

Yes. You can process your audio files without internet. Only the check for a new version needs a connection. There is no offline period like for purchased licenses.

How do I uninstall Audio Normaliser?

Mac: Quit the app and drag Audio Normaliser from the Applications folder to the Trash. Windows: Open Settings → Apps, select Audio Normaliser and click Uninstall. Your audio files stay unchanged.

Which version of Audio Normaliser is available?

As of 4 November 2026, Audio Normaliser is offered in these versions: Mac: 1.9.0; Windows: 1.9.0. This is the version of the offered download and may differ from the version installed on your computer. Find the latest available download at https://plugins.secret.vip/download/.
