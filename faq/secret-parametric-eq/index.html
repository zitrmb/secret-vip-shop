Secret Parametric EQ

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Parametric EQ do?

It lets you shape the sound of your tracks with an equalizer.

How do I install Secret Parametric EQ?

The update list includes the product but does not link to a dedicated installer. Ask support for the appropriate package. The listing alone does not confirm an available download.

What license do I need for Secret Parametric EQ?

An entry in the update list does not activate the plugin. A usable activation is not confirmed for the version documented here. Ask support before relying on the plugin.

How do I update Secret Parametric EQ?

The update list includes the product without a dedicated installer. Ask support for a released update; the listing alone is not enough to install it.

How do I report a problem with Secret Parametric EQ?

Contact our support at support@secret.vip. Include Secret Parametric EQ, the version, your operating system and what happened. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files.

What does Secret Parametric EQ require?

The product documentation specifies macOS 12 or later. A public installer and minimum Windows requirements are not confirmed here. Confirm the appropriate package before installing.
