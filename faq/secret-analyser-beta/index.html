Secret Analyser Beta

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Analyser Beta do?

It helps you assess your mix and compare tracks clearly. This help covers the beta, not the announced new free plugin.

How do I install Secret Analyser Beta?

Open the Mac installer from the Secret Analyser Beta update notice. Close your music software before installation and restart it afterwards.

What license do I need for Secret Analyser Beta?

The public free-product page does not make a promise about the beta. Activation instructions for the shipped beta package are not confirmed here. Ask support if the package requests activation.

How do I update Secret Analyser Beta?

Use the beta update notice. Install the package it offers and restart your music software.

How do I report a problem with Secret Analyser Beta?

Contact our support at support@secret.vip. Include Secret Analyser Beta, the version, your operating system and what happened. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files.

What does Secret Analyser Beta require?

The product documentation describes a Mac plugin in AU and VST3 formats for Apple Silicon and Intel. The update list offers a Mac package. The exact minimum operating system version and Windows support for this package are not confirmed here.
