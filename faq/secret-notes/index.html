Secret Notes

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Notes do?

Secret Notes shows one note from notes.secret.vip per project, right inside your DAW. You can edit it there too. Your audio stays unchanged.

What do I need for Secret Notes?

On Mac you need macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel and a DAW supporting VST3, AU or CLAP. The Windows package contains 64-bit VST3 and CLAP plugins; the interface requires Microsoft Edge WebView2. To sign in, you need a notes.secret.vip account. The minimum supported Windows version has not yet been confirmed for this download package.

Why does Secret Notes not pick the note by itself?

The note follows your project name once your project is saved. If your DAW does not report a name, pick the note yourself: use the gear menu or click into the empty text field. This happens for example in Logic when the AU plugin runs in its own process.

Do I need a purchase, a license key or an account?

Secret Notes is free. No purchase or license key is needed to download it. You need a notes.secret.vip account to sync your notes. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

How do I install Secret Notes on Mac or Windows?

Mac: Open the downloaded installer package and follow its steps. Windows: Unzip the download. Copy the complete VST3\SecretNotes.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and the CLAP\SecretNotes.clap file to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP. Restart your DAW and look for Secret Notes. Keep security protection enabled. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

How do I update Secret Notes and get help?

Get new packages from our download page. Install the package for your computer. If you need help, tell support your operating system, DAW and the version you use. Do not send passwords, sign-in details or private notes. Purchase license-key questions apply to the paid plugins. Download: https://plugins.secret.vip/download/

Is Secret Notes a trial version?

No. Secret Notes is free and not a time-limited trial. You do not need a purchase or a license key.

Can I use Secret Notes on a second computer?

Yes. Secret Notes does not need activation. Install it on the other computer and sign in there with your notes.secret.vip account. Your notes are then available there too. The two-computer limit only applies to purchased licenses.

Does Secret Notes work without internet?

You need internet to sign in. If you are signed in and the connection drops, just keep writing: your changes stay saved on your computer and are synced once you are back online. There is no offline period here, because Secret Notes does not need a purchased license.

How do I uninstall Secret Notes?

Close your DAW and delete the SecretNotes plugin files. Mac: In the Finder, open your hard drive, then Library → Audio → Plug-Ins; the files are in the VST3, Components and CLAP folders. Windows: in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP. The notes in your account stay.

Which version of Secret Notes is available?

As of 4 November 2026, Secret Notes is offered in these versions: Mac: 1.2.1; Windows: 1.2.1. This is the version of the offered download and may differ from the version installed on your computer. Find the latest available download at https://plugins.secret.vip/download/.
