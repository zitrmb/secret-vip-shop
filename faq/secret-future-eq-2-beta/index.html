Secret Future EQ 2 Beta

Short answers to the most common questions.

What does Secret Future EQ 2 Beta do?

It lets you shape the sound of your music. It is a separate test version of Secret Future EQ 2.

How do I install Secret Future EQ 2 Beta?

Use the Mac package from your beta update notice. Close your music software before installing and restart it afterwards. Select the beta when you want to test it.

What license do I need for Secret Future EQ 2 Beta?

The tester release belongs to Secret Future EQ 2. Follow the activation in the package you received using your valid license for that product. Do not include your license key in a problem report.

How do I update Secret Future EQ 2 Beta?

Use the beta update notice. The beta package and the regular release are separate versions. Install the appropriate beta package and restart your music software.

How do I report a problem with Secret Future EQ 2 Beta?

Contact our support at support@secret.vip. Include Secret Future EQ 2 Beta, the version, your operating system and what happened. Do not include license keys, passwords or private audio files.

What does Secret Future EQ 2 Beta require?

The beta feed lists a Mac installer. The exact minimum macOS version, supported processors and Windows support for this beta package are not confirmed here. Check the information supplied with your package or ask support.
