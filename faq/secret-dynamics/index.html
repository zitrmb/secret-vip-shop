Secret Dynamics

Short answers to the most common questions.

What can Secret Dynamics change about the sound?

Your sound can feel rounder and softer or harder and denser. You choose how noticeable the change is.

Can the effect stay subtle?

Yes. The strength is adjustable. You can ease back when you only want a small change.

Can my sound feel softer?

Yes. A round and soft character is one of the sound choices. How clearly you hear it also depends on the strength of the effect.

Can my sound feel harder and denser?

Yes. You can choose a harder and denser character and adjust its strength by ear.

Is there a clear sound character too?

Yes. Alongside round and soft and hard and dense, there is also a loud and clean sound character.

Can I judge the sound at a similar volume?

Yes. Automatic volume matching helps you compare. This lets you focus on the change in sound.

Can I hear the unchanged sound again?

Yes. You can turn the effect off and hear the unchanged sound.

Can the sound feel warmer too?

Yes. A rounder sound with warmth is one of the available characters.

Can the sound feel gentle or strong?

Yes. You can choose a gentle or strong feel.

Can the effect ease off quickly or more gradually?

Yes. The effect can ease off quickly or more gradually. You choose the feel that suits your music.
