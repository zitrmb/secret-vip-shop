What you get

What it does for your track

  1. Secret Match

    Pick a reference, play your track, press one button. 26 bands move your sound towards it. Amount sets how far, Match % shows how close you are.

  2. Any reference you like

    Start with the built-in Secret Curve, load or drop an audio file, or capture a sound while it plays. Keep your favourites in My Curves.

  3. Your own bands

    Double-click in the graph. Bell, shelf, cut, notch, band pass or tilt. Each band can be dynamic, soloed, or work on left, right, mid or side.

  4. Dynamic Match

    Keeps your sound close to the reference while the music plays. Set range and speed, or tame harsh S sounds in a chosen area.

  5. Decide by ear, not by volume

    Auto-Gain compensates for loudness changes, so bypass becomes a fair comparison. Three slots A / B / C, with undo and redo.

  6. Calm and clear

    Analyzer before and after the EQ, LUFS and peak meters, drive and saturation. Rest the mouse on anything to see what it does.
