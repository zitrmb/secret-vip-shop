Why producers use it

What it does for your track

  1. The tone you love, on your own track

    Pick a reference, play your track, press Secret Match. Your track moves towards the reference, and two controls decide how far and how smooth.

  2. Decide by ear, not by volume

    Three sound slots, A, B and C. Auto-Gain evens out the loudness between them, so the louder version never wins by accident.

  3. Your own moves on top

    Click in the graph to add a bell, shelf or cut. Drag for frequency and gain, scroll for width – on top of the match or on its own.

  4. References one click away

    Drop in an audio file or capture any sound live, and keep your favourite references ready for the next session.
