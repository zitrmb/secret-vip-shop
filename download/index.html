Install Secret Future EQ 2

  1. Mac: open the .pkg and follow the steps.
  2. Windows: unzip, copy VST3\SecretFutureEQ2.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and CLAP\SecretFutureEQ2.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP.
  3. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins.
  4. Open the plugin, paste your key and press Activate.
