3a. Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies on this website. Some functions store entries in your browser's storage, never a license key:

  • Light or dark display: only if you switch it yourself, your choice is kept in your browser's local storage (svp-theme) until you delete it.
  • Purchase and library: after a purchase, and when you open the download link from your email, the access link and the order number are kept in the session storage of this browser tab, so that the page still works after reloading. Access link and order number belong to your purchase and are therefore personal data; the page only uses them to fetch your order from our license server (section 6). They are deleted when you close the tab.
  • Secret Notes (page /design/): if you use Secret Notes in the browser, this page keeps its own settings (e.g. the view), your Secret Notes sign-in (email address, name, sign-in token) and the text of the note you last edited in your browser's local storage. The page only uses these entries to provide Secret Notes to you. They stay until you delete them in your browser. Your account and your notes are processed by the Secret Notes service on its own server; its own privacy policy explains this.

Display choice, access link and the Secret Notes entries are strictly necessary for functions you asked for (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is needed for them. You can delete all entries at any time in your browser settings.

5. Purchase source (link tags)

Links in our posts (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) may carry a tag in the address (e.g. ?utm_source=instagram&utm_content=clip12). It only shows which post you came from, not who you are. We use no pixels for this and do not recognise you again. What: only the tags utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content and ref from the address of the page you are viewing. To whom: when you click Buy, we pass them to Paddle; Paddle stores them with the order and sends them to us with the buyer data (section 4). If you first follow a link to another page of this website, the page appends the tags to that link; never to links to other providers. Why: we only evaluate them in aggregate, as purchases per channel and per post. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing which posts work). If you do not want this, open the page without the tag.

6. License server

Our license server license.secret.vip runs at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, in Germany (processor, Art. 28 GDPR). It only stores what the license needs:

  • License: name, email address, product, order number, time; the license key only as a one-way hash plus a short recognition part.
  • Activations: pseudonymous computer ID (one-way hash), computer type (e.g. "MacIntel"), first and last check. The email address only if you enter it when activating.
  • Trial versions (where offered): pseudonymous computer ID and trial period, so that a trial starts only once.
  • Event log: time, type (e.g. activation, deactivation), license and computer ID.

To prevent trial-license abuse, we process the IP address when a new trial is requested. New trial events store a non-reversible verification value for each day and product instead of the IP address itself. This enforces the limit of two new trials per address, day and product. The verification value is pseudonymous data. Purpose and legal basis: our legitimate interest in preventing repeated trial abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete every verification value automatically after 7 days; the trial event remains without it. Older trial events contained the IP address instead of a verification value; we remove these plain-text addresses automatically (entries from the last 7 days get the verification value instead), the event itself remains. The license server writes no access logs with IP addresses. Purposes for the other license data: performing the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and protecting license keys against misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention of the other license data: as long as the license exists, because you may use it without time limit. We keep trial data per computer for as long as we offer the product, so that a trial does not start again. We keep the event log as long as the license exists; once it ends (e.g. through a refund or blocking) or a trial expires, we delete the entries automatically after three years, counted from the end of that year. Claims under the license agreement can be raised until then (regular limitation period, sections 195 and 199 German Civil Code; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). On request we delete name, email address and activations earlier; the key is then blocked. Daily backups of the database are kept on the same server and deleted after 30 days.

We process your text, the type of report (bug or wish), product, version, operating system, audio program and plugin format and, only if you enter it, your email address for follow-up questions. Purpose: fixing bugs and improving the software; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in this (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep reports as long as we need them for handling and product improvement. We only use your email address for questions about this report and delete it at any time on request.
